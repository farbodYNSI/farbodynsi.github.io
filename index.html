About Me

I am a B.Sc. graduate in Electrical Engineering from Shahid Beheshti (Melli) University, with a focus on Control Systems. My research interests include robotics, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), autonomous vehicles, cyber-physical systems, and computer vision. I have four years of experience developing small-scale autonomous vehicles, particularly as a member of the Auriga Robotics team.

I am currently working remotely as a research assistant with Prof. Amr Youssef and Prof. Walter Lucia at Concordia University, conducting research in the field of cyber-physical systems. In addition, I serve as the Head of the Robotics Department at Danesh Institute, where I lead robotics education initiatives and mentor students in robotics projects and competitions.
