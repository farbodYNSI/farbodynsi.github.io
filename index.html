About Me

I’m an electrical engineer specializing in robotics and control systems. My work focuses on how autonomous systems perceive their environment, estimate their position, and translate sensor data into reliable motion. I combine control design, computer vision, localization and mapping, and embedded hardware and software to develop and evaluate robotic systems.

I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Shahid Beheshti (Melli) University, with a focus on control systems. I spent four years developing small-scale autonomous vehicles, including work with Auriga Robotics on lane detection, localization, and vehicle control.

I am currently working remotely as a research assistant with Prof. Amr Youssef and Prof. Walter Lucia at Concordia University, conducting research in the field of cyber-physical systems. In addition, I serve as the Head of the Robotics Department at Danesh Institute, where I lead robotics education initiatives and mentor students in robotics projects and competitions.
