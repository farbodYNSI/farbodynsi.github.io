Where we could run a pilot

Farming

Prototype a camera system for crop scouting or produce grading. Compare its counts or grading decisions with a manual sample under field lighting.

Medical & laboratory devices

Explore a benchtop imaging or positioning device with a device maker or lab. Start with repeatability on test samples.

Machine vision

Trial a compact camera station for defect inspection, part identification, or sorting. Measure detection accuracy against examples from your process.

Warehouses

Test mobile barcode capture or shelf inspection in one aisle. Track scan coverage, missed labels, and time per pass.

In development

3D scanner

A device for capturing object geometry. A useful first test is scanning a partner’s sample parts and measuring how consistently it reproduces their dimensions.

Serial servo motor

A servo motor project with a serial control interface for robotic joints and fixtures. A bench test could check repeatability and behavior under a representative load.

Arduino UNO Q camera shield

A camera add-on project for the Arduino UNO Q. A pilot could evaluate image capture on a real inspection task with your lighting and working distance.
