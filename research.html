Trojan Attacks on Neural Network Controllers for Robotic Systems

Conference on Control Technology and Applications (CCTA) 2026 Published

Farbod Younesi, Walter Lucia, Amr Youssef

Farbod Younesi, Walter Lucia, and Amr Youssef used a simulated differential-drive robot to study backdoors in neural tracking controllers. Their IEEE CCTA 2026 paper shows how a Trojan can stay dormant during normal operation and alter wheel-speed commands when a specific combination of robot pose and goal parameters triggers it.

Encrypted Cloud-Based Neural Network Controller for Differential-Drive Robots

American Control Conference (ACC) 2027 Under review

The robot encrypts its tracking inputs with CKKS fully homomorphic encryption (FHE), allowing a cloud server to compute wheel commands with a neural network while the data stays encrypted. The Webots study compares tracking performance and computation time with a classical Kanayama controller and a plaintext neural network.
